**Full description** (4000 max):

```
BizSwap Gurgaon is an AI-native marketplace for buying and selling running businesses in Gurgaon (Gurugram): pubs and bars, restaurants and cafés, doctor clinics, and CA and CS practices.

NO FORMS. JUST TALK.
Sellers simply say or type something like:
"I want to sell my pub at Ambience Mall. Footfall 100 a day, daily billing ₹10,000, lease 3 more years at ₹30,000 a month."
Our AI assistant understands it, asks only for anything that's missing, and publishes an anonymised listing with an indicative valuation, all in under a minute.

BUYERS GET MATCHED AUTOMATICALLY
Say what you're looking for: "A CA practice with 50+ clients and ₹2 lakh annual billing, budget ₹5 lakh". Our matchmaker scores every listing for fit (0–100) and explains why. Turn on "watch" and we'll tell you when a new match appears.

UBER-STYLE MAP
See every business for sale on a map of Gurgaon: Cyber Hub, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, MG Road, DLF Phases, Sector 29 and more. Filter by pubs, restaurants, clinics, CA or CS practices, and search by area or mall.

TRACK EVERY DEAL
Seller and buyer dashboards show each deal's stage: Listed → Matched → Contacted → In Discussion → Closed. You can see views, matched buyers and interest at a glance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up and no permissions. The microphone is used only when you tap it.
• Listings never show your name, phone or exact address, and map pins are offset.
• Sharing your mobile or email is optional.
• Delete all your data in one tap from Settings.

All prices are shown in ₹ lakh and crore. Valuations are indicative AI estimates, not financial advice. Demo listings marked "fictional" are sample data.
```
