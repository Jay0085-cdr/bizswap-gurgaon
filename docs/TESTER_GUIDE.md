# BizSwap Gurgaon: Tester guide 🧪

Thanks for helping! BizSwap is an app for **buying and selling running businesses** (pubs, restaurants, clinics, CA/CS practices) in Gurgaon. There are **no forms**: you just talk or type, and the AI does the rest. You're testing **version 1.0**. Your feedback decides what goes into **1.1**.

## 1. Join (2 minutes, one time)
1. Join the Google Group: **[GROUP LINK]**. Use the **same Gmail you use for Play Store** on your phone.
2. Open the testing link **on your Android phone**: **[OPT-IN LINK]**, then tap **Become a tester**.
3. Tap **Download it on Google Play** and install **BizSwap Gurgaon**. It can take up to an hour after joining before the app appears.

> No Android phone? Try the web version at https://jay0085-cdr.github.io/bizswap-gurgaon/. It works the same, except voice. But for the test to count, Android testers **must** install from Play Store.

## 2. Keep it installed for 14 days ⏳
Google only counts you if the app **stays installed for 14 days in a row**. Please **don't uninstall** and **don't leave the testing program**. Opening it **every day or two** for a couple of minutes helps a lot.

## 3. What to try (one thing per visit is enough)
All businesses on the map are **fictional demo data**, so you can't break anything.

| Visit | Try this |
|---|---|
| **Sell** | Tap **Sell a Business**, then tap 🎙 and say *"I want to sell my pub at Ambience Mall, footfall 100 a day, daily billing 10,000 rupees, lease 3 years at 30,000 a month."* Answer its questions, then tap **Publish**. |
| **Buy** | Tap **Buy**, then say *"I want to buy a CA practice in Gurgaon with 50 clients, annual billing 2 lakh, budget 5 lakh."* Then tap **Find matches**. |
| **Map** | On **Explore**: tap pins, use the filter chips, search "Cyber Hub" or "Sohna Road", then open a listing and tap **I'm interested**. |
| **Dashboard** | Check **Dashboard** and **Inbox**. Do you understand the stages (Listed → Matched → Contacted…)? |
| **Your own idea** | Describe a made-up business **in your own words**: Hinglish, short or messy is fine. Does the AI understand? |

If you see **"AI is busy, using quick mode"**, that's expected. We use a free AI tier, so it just means the answers may be simpler for a moment.

## 4. Send feedback 💬
Tap the **💬 speech-bubble icon** at the top right of any screen. Type or speak. Good feedback says:
- **what you did** → **what happened** → **what you expected**.
- Anything confusing, slow, ugly or broken is useful. So is what you liked.

Please **don't** type real phone numbers, names or other private info in the chats.

**Questions?** Ask in the WhatsApp group or email jayvasani8112005@gmail.com.
